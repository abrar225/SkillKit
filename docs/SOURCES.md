## License and mirroring policy

- **Freely redistributable with attribution (or public-domain) SPDX allowlist:** `0BSD`, `Apache-2.0`, `BSD-2-Clause`, `BSD-3-Clause`, `BSL-1.0` (Boost Software License 1.0), `CC0-1.0`, `CC-BY-4.0`, `ISC`, `MIT`, `MIT-0`, `Unlicense`, `Zlib`. `BUSL-1.1` (Business Source License 1.1), copyleft (`GPL`/`AGPL`), non-commercial, and proprietary licenses are excluded.
- **Skill-directory verification:** Auto-verified Atlas entries require skill-directory-level license evidence (a `LICENSE*`/`COPYING*` file in the skill directory or an explicit permissive `license:` field in `SKILL.md` frontmatter) with full text parsing and spot-check verification on mixed-license repositories. Repo-root hints alone or uncertain terms remain `metadata-only` links and are never delivered or proxied, even upon explicit request.
- **Text-only mirroring & trust tiers:** Auto-verified Atlas entries sit in their own `auto-verified` tier below `curated` (`registry/skills.json`) and individually `reviewed` pins. Mirroring stores `SKILL.md`, non-executable license/notice files, and non-executable text references only. Bundled scripts and executables are never mirrored or executed. Static scans block high-risk patterns and quarantine drifted or failing pins.
